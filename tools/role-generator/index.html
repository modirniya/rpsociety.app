Mafia role generator

Set the table, then pass the phone: each player reveals their own role in secret, and the mafia see their team. Then let it run the night. It deals from the same distribution the game uses and resolves the night by the same rules — and nothing ever leaves your browser.

How the deal works

  1. Set up the table. Pick how many are playing — names are optional. You get the game's own deal for that size, or open it up and change the roles; the tool tells you when a table is unbalanced and why, and never stops you.
  2. Pass the phone. The screen shows only whose turn it is. That person taps to reveal their card, it hides itself a few seconds later, and they hand it on. Mafia see who their team is, exactly as the game's first night would show them.
  3. Narrate. The tool reads the night in the right order, with only the roles actually at the table, records what each of them did, and works out the morning. Whoever holds the phone is the narrator, and they never have to remember anything.

What it will not do

It will not send your table anywhere, ask you to sign in, or forget your group between games — the last table you set up is remembered on this device and nowhere else. Turn the page into an app from your browser's menu and it keeps working with no signal at all.

Or skip the paper entirely

Everyone in one room? These tools are for you. Apart? The game itself deals in secret, runs the night, resolves every action and tells each player only what they should know — so nobody has to sit out and narrate. Same roles, same rules.
