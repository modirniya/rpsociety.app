Mafia with 12 players

The only game with a fourth mafioso, and the only one with Dr. Lecter. He is not a killer — the team still lands three killers' worth of threat and gains a medic who shields them from the Sniper.

How it plays

The full game. Every role in the production set is on the table at once, and the Doctor starts on two saves a night.

The mafia land one kill a night, and only three of the four can take it — Dr. Lecter protects rather than kills. The town wins by eliminating all four mafia; the mafia win once the living town is down to four.

The Doctor protects two people a night while ten or more are alive, dropping to one below that.
