Citizen

No power, no information, and the hardest job at the table.

Side
Town — wins when every mafioso is gone.
Appears at
Between one and four of them, depending on the size of the game.
At night
Nothing at all. Citizens sleep through every night and learn what happened when everyone else does.
During the day
Everything. A Citizen's only instruments are the argument, the vote, and what he can infer from who is pushing whom.

The thing people get wrong

Being useless at night is what makes the Citizen useful during the day. He has nothing to protect and no result to defend, so he can push hard on a suspicion that a Doctor or Detective would have to be careful about. He is also the only role a mafioso can claim without immediately being asked for evidence.

Where it turns up

Games with this role: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 players. See the exact composition at each size in setups by player count, or read the full rules.
